How Our Book Design & KDP Publishing Consultation Works

Whether you are preparing your very first manuscript for Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) or managing a multi-book series launch, reaching out to Hamid Raza at Loufy Publisher is simple and stress-free. Once you submit your project details through the contact form above or message us directly via email or WhatsApp, we review your genre, word count, trim size, and target publishing platforms within 12 to 24 hours. We provide a clear, no-obligation project roadmap recommending the best option from our book publishing pricing packages or crafting a custom bundle tailored to your exact timeline and budget.

Manuscript & Artwork Preparation Checklist

Pre-Submission Guide

To help us give you the most accurate quote and fastest turnaround for book cover design or interior formatting, here is what helps to share when you reach out:

  • For Interior Formatting: Your final edited manuscript in Microsoft Word (.docx) or Google Docs, approximate word count, preferred trim size (e.g., 6" × 9" or 5.5" × 8.5"), and whether your book contains images, tables, or footnotes.
  • For Book Cover Design: Your book title, subtitle, author name, back-cover blurb, target genre, and 2–3 reference covers from our portfolio or Amazon that match your visual style.
  • For Author Websites: The number of books you want to showcase, domain preferences, and any required features like newsletter signups or direct store links.

Supported Self-Publishing Platforms & Turnaround Times

Platform Compliance

We format and design print-ready PDFs, full-wrap covers, and validated EPUBs for every major global self-publishing distributor:

  • Amazon KDP: Kindle eBook, paperback, hardcover case-laminate, and Amazon A+ Content modules.
  • IngramSpark & Wide Retailers: Barnes & Noble Press, Apple Books, Rakuten Kobo, Google Play Books, Draft2Digital, Lulu, and BookBaby.
  • Fast Delivery: Standard delivery is 3–5 business days, with 24–48 hour express turnaround available for urgent book launches. While you wait, test your existing cover ideas in our 3D Book Mockup Generator.

Common Questions Before Starting Your Book Project

FAQ & Guarantees
  • What if I don't know my final page count yet? No problem! When we format your interior layout first, we lock in the exact final page count so we can calculate the precise spine width for your paperback and hardcover full-wrap covers without any guesswork. If you only need cover design right now, we can design your front eBook cover first and generate the print spine wrap as soon as your manuscript layout is complete.
  • What happens if Amazon KDP or IngramSpark flags a print file error? Every package includes our 100% Print Approval Guarantee. Because we preflight every print-ready PDF and reflowable EPUB against official Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, Lulu, and Draft2Digital technical specifications—verifying 0.125-inch bleed margins, embedded fonts, 300 DPI CMYK ink density, and spine fold tolerances—our files pass review on the first upload. If a platform ever requests an adjustment, we update and re-deliver your files immediately at zero extra cost.
  • Do I retain 100% of my book royalties and copyright ownership? Absolutely. Unlike vanity presses that take a percentage of your book sales or lock your files behind proprietary accounts, Loufy Publisher operates strictly as a fee-for-service creative studio. You retain 100% of your intellectual property, source files, ISBN rights, and author royalties across every retail channel.
  • Can I see verified client results before hiring? Yes—browse our verified client reviews & delivered project samples or explore our self-publishing blog for step-by-step KDP tutorials.
