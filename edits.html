How Loufy AI Video & Caption Studio Works

1. Upload & Audio Extraction: Your video stays 100% private in your browser. Web Audio API extracts the waveform and 16kHz mono PCM track.

2. Whisper AI Word Timestamps: Local Transformers.js Whisper transcribes speech and computes millisecond timestamps for every single word.

3. Smart Cuts: Energy-based dB analysis finds long pauses and filler words ("um", "uh", repeated takes) and skips them seamlessly during playback & export.

4. 7 Kinetic Styles & Beats: Pick from Warm Canvas, Creator Motion, Clean Tech, Software Walk, Data Story, Chalk Talk, or Pop Info. Captions automatically hide during Motion Beats.

5. Export: Download the rendered video with burned-in captions, or export .ASS, .SRT, .VTT, and transcript_edited.json.
