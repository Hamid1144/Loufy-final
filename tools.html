How to Create Mockups

1. Select a Template: Choose any of the 10 professional 3D mockups from the left drawer.

2. Upload Artwork: In the right inspector panel, upload your Front Cover, Back Cover, or Spine image individually, or drop a full wrap dust jacket to auto-slice.

3. Pick a Background: Select transparent PNG, studio lighting, or real wood desk and concrete textures.

4. Fine-Tune & Export: Switch to the Adjust tab on the left to tweak lighting and shadows, then click Export Mockup.
