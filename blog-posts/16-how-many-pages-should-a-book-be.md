# How Many Pages Should a Book Be? Word Count by Genre & Print Guide (2026)

When you are deep in the trenches of drafting your manuscript, one question inevitably haunts every author's mind: *Is my book too short? Is it too long?* First-time authors often obsess over page counts, glancing nervously at the page counter in Microsoft Word or Scrivener. But in modern commercial publishing, page count is a deceptive metric: font selection, leading, margin widths, and trim size can cause a 75,000-word story to stretch across 240 pages or balloon past 360 pages. So **how many pages should a book be**, and what word count thresholds satisfy reader expectations in your specific genre?

In this comprehensive 2026 industry guide, we break down the definitive **word count by genre** benchmarks, clarify the dividing lines between **novella vs novel length**, review Amazon KDP and IngramSpark physical print minimums, and explain how page length directly dictates your print-on-demand manufacturing costs and royalty profit margins.

---

## 1. Words vs. Pages: Why Word Count Is the Only Reliable Metric

Before evaluating genre benchmarks, every author must understand why the publishing industry measures books by word count rather than physical pages:
- **Font Proportions**: A manuscript set in 11 pt Garamond will yield approximately 280 words per formatted book page. The same manuscript set in 12 pt Arial will yield only 210 words per page.
- **Trim Size Variables**: A 70,000-word novel printed in a compact 5.0" x 8.0" trim size will fill roughly 310 pages, while the exact same story laid out in a standard 6.0" x 9.0" non-fiction trim will compress into just 220 pages.
- **Digital eBooks**: On Kindle and Apple Books, physical pages do not exist. Text reflows dynamically based on device screens, meaning a reader's font settings determine how many screens they swipe through.

As a general rule of thumb for commercial fiction formatted at a standard 5.5" x 8.5" trim size:
**1 Formatted Book Page ≈ 250 to 280 Words**

## 2. Definitive Word Count and Page Count by Genre (2026 Benchmarks)

Commercial readers in different categories carry deeply ingrained expectations about book length. A reader paying $16.99 for an epic fantasy novel expects a substantial, immersive reading experience; conversely, someone buying a focused productivity handbook expects concise, actionable insights without unnecessary fluff.

Here are the standard industry targets for the **ideal book length** across major publishing categories:

| Publishing Genre | Target Word Count Range | Formatted Page Count (5.5" x 8.5") | Reader Expectations & Tone |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commercial Fiction & Thrillers** | 70,000 – 90,000 words | 260 – 340 pages | Fast-paced, high tension, tight chapter hooks |
| **Epic Fantasy & Sci-Fi** | 90,000 – 125,000 words | 340 – 480 pages | Expansive worldbuilding, multi-POV character arcs |
| **Contemporary Romance & Rom-Com**| 60,000 – 85,000 words | 220 – 310 pages | Banter-heavy, emotional rhythm, fast read |
| **Historical Fiction** | 85,000 – 115,000 words | 310 – 430 pages | Period authenticity, rich historical atmosphere |
| **Young Adult (YA) Fiction** | 55,000 – 75,000 words | 200 – 280 pages | Immediate pacing, high emotional stakes |
| **Middle Grade (Ages 8–12)** | 25,000 – 45,000 words | 110 – 180 pages | Accessible vocabulary, frequent chapter breaks |
| **Business, Self-Help & Non-Fiction**| 40,000 – 65,000 words | 160 – 250 pages | Tactical frameworks, case studies, zero fluff |
| **Memoir & Autobiography** | 65,000 – 85,000 words | 240 – 320 pages | Narrative arc, thematic focus, personal transformation |

## 3. Classifying Book Length: Flash Fiction, Novella, and Novel

Where does your project fit on the literary spectrum? The Science Fiction & Fantasy Writers Association (SFWA) and major publishing organizations define story lengths according to these standardized thresholds:

- **Flash Fiction**: Under 1,000 words (1 to 3 pages).
- **Short Story**: 1,000 to 7,500 words (4 to 28 pages).
- **Novelette**: 7,500 to 17,500 words (29 to 65 pages).
- **Novella**: 17,500 to 40,000 words (65 to 150 pages). Highly popular on Kindle Unlimited as reader magnets, prequel novellas, and quick series bridges.
- **Full-Length Novel**: 40,000 words and above. The standard commercial fiction format.

## 4. Amazon KDP and IngramSpark Physical Print Constraints

When self-publishing physical books through Print-on-Demand (POD), platform manufacturing rules dictate your technical boundaries:

### A. Minimum Pages for KDP Paperbacks
- **Absolute Minimum Page Count**: Amazon KDP paperbacks require a strict minimum of **24 pages**. Any manuscript under 24 pages cannot be bound into a physical paperback.
- **Spine Text Rule**: Amazon KDP prohibits spine text on paperbacks with **fewer than 79 pages**. If your formatted book is between 24 and 78 pages, your spine must remain completely blank.

### B. Minimum Pages for Hardcover Editions
- **Amazon KDP Hardcover**: Requires a minimum of **72 pages** (maximum 828 pages).
- **IngramSpark Hardcover**: Case laminate hardcovers typically require a minimum of **18 to 24 pages** depending on the specific binding and paper weight selected.

### C. Maximum Page Limits
On Amazon KDP, the maximum page limit for standard black-and-white paperbacks is **828 pages**. For books exceeding this threshold (such as massive multi-book fantasy omnibuses), the work must be split into two separate volumes to accommodate physical glue binding machines.

## 5. The Economics of Page Count: Printing Costs vs. Author Royalties

In traditional publishing, page count impacts paper purchasing contracts. In self-publishing on Amazon KDP, page count directly impacts your manufacturing cost per book, which dictates your royalty payout on every sale.

### Amazon KDP Paperback Printing Cost Formula (US Marketplace):
$$\text{Printing Cost} = \text{Fixed Cost ($1.00)} + (\text{Page Count} \times \text{$0.013})$$

Let's compare the print economics of two novels at the same retail price of **$14.99**:

| Book Metric | Book A (65,000 words / 240 pages) | Book B (130,000 words / 480 pages) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **KDP Fixed Cost** | $1.00 | $1.00 |
| **Per-Page Cost** | 240 x $0.013 = $3.12 | 480 x $0.013 = $6.24 |
| **Total Printing Cost** | **$4.12** | **$7.24** |
| **Retail List Price** | $14.99 | $14.99 |
| **Amazon 60% Gross Share** | $8.99 | $8.99 |
| **Author Net Royalty Per Sale** | **$4.87 ($8.99 - $4.12)** | **$1.75 ($8.99 - $7.24)** |

As shown above, an author publishing a 480-page tome earns less than half the royalty of an author publishing a tight 240-page book at the same retail price point. If your book runs long, you must either raise your retail price (e.g., to $17.99 or $18.99) or work with a developmental editor to trim redundant scenes and protect your profit margins.

## Frequently Asked Questions (PAA)

### How many pages is a 50,000 word book?
A 50,000-word manuscript formatted for a standard commercial paperback (5.5" x 8.5" trim size with 11 pt font and 1.2x line spacing) typically results in approximately 180 to 200 formatted book pages.

### What is the ideal page count for a first-time novel?
For a debut commercial novel, the ideal sweet spot is between 250 and 320 formatted pages (approximately 70,000 to 85,000 words). This length satisfies reader expectations for a complete, satisfying story without ballooning print costs or triggering buyer fatigue.

### What is the minimum page count to publish a paperback on Amazon KDP?
The minimum page count required to publish a paperback on Amazon KDP is 24 pages. However, to print text on the spine of the book, the manuscript must be at least 79 pages long.
