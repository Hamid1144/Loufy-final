# How to Create Amazon A+ Content for Books: The Step-by-Step KDP Walkthrough (2026)

When shoppers browse Amazon for their next read, they do not just read the blurb—they judge the entire visual presentation of your product page. Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing's A+ Content feature allows independent authors to transform drab, text-only product pages into rich, magazine-style storefronts featuring cinematic artwork, character spotlights, reader quotes, and series reading orders. But if you have never navigated Amazon's A+ Content Manager before, the process can feel confusing: which modules should you stack? What exact pixel dimensions are required? How do you link your eBook, paperback, and hardcover editions simultaneously? In this practical, click-by-click tutorial on **how to create amazon a+ content for books**, we guide you through the entire production and submission process in 2026.

Whether you are seeking a reliable **a+ content template**, looking for exact **a+ content requirements**, or mastering **amazon book listing optimization**, this comprehensive guide will help you build and publish a high-converting layout with guaranteed zero-rejection approval.

---

## 1. Step 1: Navigating to the KDP A+ Content Manager

Accessing Amazon's visual merchandising portal is simple once you know where to look:

1. Log into your **Amazon KDP dashboard** at **kdp.amazon.com**.
2. In the top navigation bar, click on **Marketing**.
3. Scroll down the Marketing page until you locate the section titled **A+ Content**.
4. Choose your primary Amazon retail marketplace from the dropdown menu (e.g., *Amazon.com* for the US store).
5. Click the yellow button: **Manage A+ Content**.
6. On the A+ Content dashboard, click **Start creating A+ content** in the upper-right corner.

---

## 2. Step 2: Naming Your Project and Selecting Your Language

Before adding graphic modules, you must configure initial project metadata:
- **Content Name**: Choose a clean internal reference name (e.g., *"The Blackwood Manor - Flagship Launch A+"* or *"Shadow Series - Comparison Grid"*). This name is strictly for your personal reference and is never visible to Amazon shoppers.
- **Language**: Select your primary language (e.g., **English**). Amazon requires that your A+ Content matches the primary language of the book listing.

---

## 3. Step 3: Choosing and Stacking Your A+ Content Modules

Amazon allows you to stack up to **five individual modules** per A+ Content project. To build a cohesive, conversion-focused narrative, follow this battle-tested 4-module layout stack:

### Module 1: The Hero Billboard (Standard Image Header with Text)
- **Image Dimensions**: **970 x 600 pixels**.
- **Role**: This is your visual hook. Use a stunning full-width banner showcasing your primary world map, epic landscape, or a dramatic character composite with your book's primary tagline in bold typography.

### Module 2: The Praise & Social Proof Banner (Standard Image & Light Text Overlay)
- **Image Dimensions**: **970 x 300 pixels**.
- **Role**: A wide horizontal banner displaying 2 to 3 prestigious editorial review quotes (from trade review journals, author blurbs, or notable bloggers) over an atmospheric background texture.

### Module 3: Character / Feature Highlights (Standard Three Image & Text)
- **Image Dimensions**: **Three images at 300 x 300 pixels each**.
- **Role**: In fiction, use these three cards to introduce your primary characters (Protagonist, Love Interest, Antagonist) with short personality bios. In non-fiction, use them to highlight three core takeaways or methodologies from the book.

### Module 4: The Series Reading Order / Comparison Grid (Standard Comparison Table)
- **Image Dimensions**: **150 x 300 pixels per book cover**.
- **Role**: Display up to five books in your series. Include columns for book cover thumbnails, volume numbers, genre tropes, page counts, and direct links to their live ASIN pages.

---

## 4. Step 4: Preparing Your Graphic Assets (Resolution & Specs)

Before uploading images to Amazon, ensure all files comply with Amazon's technical **a+ content requirements**:

### Technical Asset Checklist:
- **File Format**: High-quality **JPG** or **PNG**.
- **Color Profile**: **sRGB Color Space**. (Never upload CMYK files; CMYK graphics will appear discolored or display inverted color errors on Amazon).
- **Resolution**: Design at **300 DPI** to ensure razor-sharp rendering on high-density iPhone retina displays and iPad screens.
- **File Size**: Keep individual image files under **3 MB** each.
- **Image Keywords (Alt Text)**: Amazon requires you to provide alt text for every uploaded image. Use descriptive, keyword-rich phrases explaining the image (e.g., *"Custom fantasy map showing the realm of Eldoria for epic fantasy novel"*). This boosts accessibility and improves search engine indexing.

---

## 5. Step 5: Applying ASINs (Linking Your Book Formats)

Once your modules are arranged and images are uploaded, you must assign your project to your live book listings:

1. Click the **Next: Apply ASINs** button in the upper-right corner.
2. In the "Add ASINs" search box, search for your book by title or enter its specific 10-digit **ASIN** (or 13-digit paperback ISBN).
3. **Crucial Pro Tip**: Amazon treats your Kindle eBook, paperback, and hardcover editions as separate ASINs. **Apply your A+ Content to all three ASINs simultaneously**. This ensures that whichever edition a shopper views, your visual A+ Content displays seamlessly.
4. If you created a series-wide comparison banner, you can add all ASINs across your entire backlist to the same project!
5. Click **Apply content**.

---

## 6. Step 6: Reviewing and Submitting for Amazon Approval

Before submitting, click the **Preview** button in the upper right:

### Mobile Preview Check:
Toggle to the **Mobile** view tab. Over 60% of Amazon traffic is mobile. Verify that:
- Any text embedded inside your graphics remains legible on a smartphone screen.
- Image headers scale down cleanly without awkward cropping.

### Submitting to Amazon:
Click **Submit for approval**. Your project status will change to "Submitted." Amazon's automated review system will inspect your content for policy compliance:
- **Approval Window**: Most submissions are approved within **24 to 72 hours**.
- **Once Approved**: Your A+ Content will automatically go live on your Amazon product detail pages under the "From the Publisher" header.
- **If Rejected**: Amazon will send an email detailing the specific violation (e.g., a pricing claim or an unauthorized customer quote). Simply edit the offending graphic and resubmit.

## Frequently Asked Questions (PAA)

### How many modules can you have in Amazon A+ Content?
Amazon allows you to add up to five modules per A+ Content project. You can mix and match any combination of header banners, single image blocks, multiple image grids, and comparison tables to create your custom layout.

### Do I need to create separate A+ Content for Kindle and paperback?
No. You can create a single A+ Content project and apply it to both your Kindle eBook ASIN and your physical paperback and hardcover ISBNs simultaneously, ensuring uniform visual branding across all editions.

### Why was my Amazon A+ Content rejected?
Common reasons for rejection include referencing Amazon customer reviews, mentioning pricing or discounts ("free," "cheap"), using time-sensitive phrases ("new release," "pre-order"), making unsubstantiated bestseller claims, or including external website URLs.
