# How to Publish a Book on Amazon KDP Step by Step: The Complete 2026 Checklist

Publishing your book on Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) is one of the most empowering milestones in an author's life. With a few clicks, your manuscript can be accessible to hundreds of millions of readers across the United States, the UK, Europe, Japan, and Australia. But logging into the KDP dashboard for the first time can feel overwhelming: there are dozens of input fields, cryptic tax interviews, royalty percentage options, territory checkboxes, and strict file preview warnings. If you select the wrong setting or upload non-compliant files, your book can be delayed in review or launched with broken formatting. In this practical, click-by-click tutorial on **how to publish a book on amazon kdp step by step**, we walk you through the entire **kdp publishing process** from start to finish.

Whether you are complete newcomer exploring **amazon kdp for beginners** or preparing to **upload book to kdp** for a major series launch, use this comprehensive walkthrough as your bulletproof **kdp publishing checklist**.

---

## 1. Step 1: KDP Account Setup and Tax Interview

Before you can upload files, you must configure your author publisher profile:

1. Visit **kdp.amazon.com** and sign in with your existing Amazon account (or create a dedicated author account).
2. Click **Your Account** in the top navigation banner.
3. Complete the three mandatory profile sections:
   - **Author / Publisher Information**: Enter your legal name, physical address, and contact phone number. (Note: You can publish under a pseudonym/pen name later; this section is strictly for Amazon's legal records).
   - **Bank Information**: Add your bank routing and account number to receive direct deposit electronic royalty payments every month.
   - **Tax Information (Interview)**: Complete the automated W-9 / W-8BEN tax questionnaire. If you are a US citizen, enter your SSN or EIN. If you are an international author, enter your local foreign tax identification number to claim applicable tax treaty rate reductions.
4. Click **Save** to activate publishing privileges.

---

## 2. Step 2: Creating Your Title and Entering Book Details (Metadata)

From your KDP Bookshelf dashboard, click the vibrant yellow **+ Create** button, then select either **Kindle eBook** or **Paperback**. (You can link both editions together seamlessly once uploaded).

### Title, Subtitle, and Series Information:
- **Book Title**: Must match the exact wording on your cover.
- **Subtitle (Optional)**: Ideal for non-fiction keywords or series hooks (e.g., "A Gripping Psychological Suspense Thriller").
- **Series Details**: If your book is part of a series, click "Add series details" to group your books together on Amazon into an automatic 1-click series page.
- **Edition Number**: Leave blank for first editions; use only if releasing a revised second edition.

### Author Name and Contributors:
- **Primary Author**: Enter your real name or your chosen pen name.
- **Contributors**: Add your editor, illustrator, or translator if desired.

### Crafting a High-Converting Book Description:
Amazon allows rich HTML formatting in the description box. Maximize conversions by utilizing:
- **Bold Headlines (`<b>...</b>`)**: To hook the browser in the first sentence.
- **Italicized Testimonials (`<i>...</i>`)**: To highlight social proof from early reviewers.
- **Bullet Points (`<ul><li>...</li></ul>`)**: Ideal for non-fiction takeaways or character trope lists.

### Publishing Rights and Age Ranges:
- Select **"I own the copyright and I hold necessary publishing rights."**
- Mark explicit content truthfully ("No" unless containing graphic erotica or extreme gore).

---

## 3. Step 3: Keywords and Categories Selection

This is where your book's organic search discoverability is won or lost:

### The 7 Backend Keyword Boxes:
Never waste space repeating words already in your title or author name. Use long-tail search phrases (up to 50 characters per box) that real readers type into the Amazon search bar:
- *Box 1*: "enemies to lovers workplace romance"
- *Box 2*: "fast paced psychological thriller with twists"
- *Box 3*: "epic fantasy books for adults with magic"
- Combine relevant terms without commas to maximize search algorithm indexing.

### Category Selection (BISAC Codes):
Amazon KDP allows you to select up to **three primary categories** directly during the setup workflow. Browse the category tree carefully to find specific, targeted sub-genres (e.g., *Fiction > Mystery & Detective > Cozy > Culinary*) where your book can realistically climb into the Top 20 bestseller rankings.

---

## 4. Step 4: Manuscript and Cover File Upload

This tab represents the technical core of your publishing setup:

### Digital Rights Management (DRM):
- For Kindle eBooks, Amazon asks whether to apply DRM. Most top indie authors select **"No"** to allow readers to easily back up their books across personal devices without DRM friction.

### Uploading Your Formatted Manuscript:
- **For Kindle eBooks**: Upload a validated **EPUB (or KPF)** file.
- **For Paperbacks**: Upload a 300 DPI, print-ready **PDF** with fonts 100% embedded and mirror margins configured.

### Uploading Your Cover Art:
- **For Kindle eBooks**: Upload a single high-resolution front cover JPEG (recommended 2,560 x 1,600 pixels).
- **For Paperbacks**: Upload a complete wraparound PDF (back cover, spine, and front cover + 0.125" bleed). Avoid Amazon's basic online "Cover Creator" tool if you want your book to look commercially professional.

### Launching the KDP Print Previewer:
Click **Launch Previewer**. Amazon will spend 1–3 minutes processing your files. Carefully inspect every page spread:
- Ensure no text violates the dotted outer trim lines or the inner gutter boundary.
- Verify that chapter headers appear properly aligned.
- Confirm barcode placement on the lower-right back cover.
- Once error-free, click **Approve** in the bottom-right corner.

---

## 5. Step 5: Pricing, Territories, and Royalties

The final tab controls your commercial distribution and royalties:

### KDP Select Enrollment (eBook Only):
- Check "Enroll my book in KDP Select" if you want your eBook available in **Kindle Unlimited (KU)**. Note that this requires making your digital edition 100% exclusive to Amazon for 90 days.

### Territory Rights:
- Select **"Worldwide rights (all territories)"** unless you have sold foreign language rights to another publishing house.

### Choosing Your Royalty Plan:
- **35% Royalty**: Required if pricing your eBook below $2.99 or above $9.99.
- **70% Royalty**: The gold standard for eBooks priced between **$2.99 and $9.99** in major territories.

### Setting Paperback Retail Pricing:
Amazon displays your exact printing cost based on page count. Set a retail price that provides a healthy royalty:
$$\text{Author Royalty} = (\text{List Price} \times 60\%) - \text{Printing Cost}$$
For instance, a 280-page paperback retailing at $14.99 with a $4.64 print cost yields **$4.35 net royalty** on every Amazon sale.

### Expanded Distribution (Paperback):
Check **Expanded Distribution** to allow academic wholesalers and online retailers to order your paperback through Ingram's wholesale catalog (earning a 40% net royalty calculation).

---

## 6. Step 6: Hit Publish and What Happens Next

Click the yellow button: **Publish Your Kindle eBook** or **Publish Your Paperback Book**.

### What to Expect Post-Submission:
1. **In Review Status**: Your book status will change to "In Review." Amazon's quality assurance team will inspect your files within **24 to 72 hours**.
2. **Live on Amazon**: Once approved, your book status changes to "Live," and you will receive an email confirmation containing your live Amazon product listing link.
3. **Ordering Author Proofs & Author Copies**: You can order wholesale author copies printed at cost (typically $3.50 to $5.50 per book plus shipping) to sell at book signings, distribute to friends, or stock at local independent bookstores.

## Frequently Asked Questions (PAA)

### How long does it take for a book to be published on Amazon KDP?
After clicking publish on the KDP dashboard, it typically takes between 24 and 72 hours for Amazon to review your files and make your Kindle eBook and paperback live on the Amazon store worldwide.

### Is publishing on Amazon KDP really free?
Yes. Creating an account, uploading manuscripts, and publishing books on Amazon KDP is 100% free. Amazon takes a percentage of sales and deducts print manufacturing costs from your royalties when copies are sold, meaning there are zero upfront platform fees.

### Can I edit my book after it is published on KDP?
Yes. You can update your manuscript interior, upload a refreshed cover, change retail pricing, or revise keywords and categories at any time directly from your KDP dashboard without paying change fees. Updates go live within 24 to 48 hours.
