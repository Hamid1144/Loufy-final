About Me

Who is Hamid Raza?

Hamid Raza is an Amazon KDP publishing expert and the founder of Loufy Publisher, a full-service studio for self-published and hybrid-published authors. He specializes in professional interior formatting — Kindle, paperback, and hardcover — along with custom book cover design and Amazon A+ Content for Author Central. Loufy Publisher also offers author website design and app development for writers and brands looking to build a stronger digital presence.

With extensive experience working with authors worldwide, Hamid helps writers turn their manuscripts into polished, market-ready books that stand out in the competitive self-publishing space — without ever compromising on professional publishing standards.

Absolutely! I specialize in colorful, engaging children's book covers and interior illustrations that captivate young readers and their parents.

Yes, all packages include source files in PSD, AI, or other editable formats so you have full ownership and flexibility with your designs.

Can you redesign old book covers? +

Of course! I frequently help authors refresh and modernize their existing book covers to boost sales and improve market appeal.

Do you create websites? +
