What Is Included in Every Book Publishing Package

Whether you require standard eBook formatting, a complete paperback jacket wrap, or a comprehensive bestseller marketing suite, every tier includes commercial licensing, print-ready files, and our 100% platform approval guarantee.

Amazon KDP, IngramSpark & Print-Ready Deliverables

Production Files

When you book any book design package through Loufy Publisher, you receive production-ready source and output assets engineered specifically for automated pre-media checks:

  • Print-Ready PDF: Formatted to your exact trim size (5x8", 5.5x8.5", 6x9", 7x10", or custom) at 300 DPI with CMYK color profiles, embedded fonts, and precise bleed margins.
  • Reflowable EPUB: Validated ePub files adhering to IDPF and W3C digital publishing specifications, featuring clickable tables of contents, active hyperlinks, and responsive imagery for Amazon Kindle, Apple Books, and Kobo.
  • Full Wrap Print Covers: Single-piece wrap files with calculated spine width, barcode placeholder, and safety margins for both paperback and case laminate hardcover.

Frequently Asked Questions About Publishing Rates & Revisions

Billing & Policies
  • What if my manuscript is over 50,000 words? Our Starter package covers standard manuscripts up to 50,000 words. For larger novels or academic non-fiction (60k–120k+ words), our Publishing Pro Suite and Bestseller Master packages easily accommodate extended layouts at a competitive per-word rate.
  • Do you guarantee Amazon KDP approval? Yes, 100%. If Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, or Barnes & Noble flags any formatting or cover dimension warning, we revise the file immediately at zero extra charge until it passes inspection smoothly.
  • How many revision rounds are included? Every publishing package includes dedicated revision rounds so you can fine-tune font pairings, chapter header ornaments, spacing, and cover typography until your book matches your exact vision.
