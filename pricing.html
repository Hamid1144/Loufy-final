Deliverables & Value

What Is Included in Every Book Publishing Package

At Loufy Publisher, our pricing packages are structured to give independent authors, self-publishers, and traditional imprints complete transparency with zero hidden fees or royalty deductions. Unlike vanity presses that take a percentage of your book sales, you retain 100% of your rights and 100% of your royalties on every platform. Whether you choose the Starter Package ($99), the Bestseller Master ($199), or our flagship Publishing Pro Suite ($299), every order is personally handled by founder Hamid Raza using industry-standard Adobe InDesign, Photoshop, and Illustrator workflows.

Every tier includes thorough pre-flight validation so your interior and cover files pass automated and manual review checks on the very first upload. You can also preview your artwork anytime using our free 3D Book Mockup Studio or browse hundreds of completed titles in our book design portfolio.

Amazon KDP, IngramSpark & Print-Ready Deliverable Specifications

Each package delivers press-ready files calibrated to your exact trim size (such as 5" × 8", 5.5" × 8.5", 6" × 9", or 8.5" × 8.5" square children's books) and paper stock (white, cream, or premium color):

  • Print-Ready Interior PDF/X-1a: Embedded fonts, precision gutter and outside margins, balanced widow/orphan control, custom running headers, and drop caps.
  • Reflowable & Fixed-Layout EPUB: Validated Kindle KDP, Apple Books, Kobo, and Draft2Digital eBook files with clickable HTML Table of Contents.
  • Full-Wrap Print Covers: 300 DPI CMYK paperback and hardcover dust-jacket/case-laminate wraps with exact spine width calculations and barcode placement.
  • Editable Source Files: Available with our comprehensive book publishing services so you always own your master assets.

Frequently Asked Questions About Publishing Rates & Revisions

What if my manuscript is over 50,000 words?

Our Publishing Pro Suite and Bestseller Master packages accommodate full-length novels and non-fiction books up to 100,000+ words. For epic fantasy novels or multi-volume box sets, contact us for a custom quote.

Do you guarantee Amazon KDP approval?

Yes! Every cover and interior file is backed by our 100% KDP & IngramSpark approval guarantee. Read our client reviews and KDP formatting guides to see how we help authors publish stress-free.
